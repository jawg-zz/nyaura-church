About Our Church

Rooted in Scripture,
united in purpose

Nyaura SDA Church is a community of believers devoted to worshiping God in spirit and truth. We hold fast to the timeless message of the Three Angels and the hope of Christ's soon return.

Whether you're exploring faith for the first time or deepening a lifelong walk, you'll find warmth, encouragement, and purpose here.
