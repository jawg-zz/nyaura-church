About Our Church

A family of believers rooted in faith

Nyaura SDA Church is a vibrant Seventh-day Adventist congregation committed to worshiping God in spirit and truth. For over 25 years, we've been a beacon of hope in our community.

We believe in the transformative power of faith, the importance of community, and the hope found in God's Word. Our doors are open to all who seek spiritual growth and meaningful fellowship.

Our Leadership

Pr. Daniel Mwangi

Senior Pastor

With a heart for ministry and a passion for sharing the Gospel, Pastor Daniel Mwangi leads our congregation with wisdom, compassion, and a genuine love for God's people.

Trained at Bugema University and ordained in 2010, Pastor Mwangi has faithfully served churches across East Africa before joining Nyaura in 2021. He and his wife Grace have three children.

"Come as you are. God has a plan for your life, and it starts with you taking the first step through those doors."
