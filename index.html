About Us

Growing Together in Christ

Nyaura SDA Church is a vibrant Seventh-day Adventist congregation committed to worshiping God in spirit and truth. We believe in the transformative power of faith, the importance of community, and the hope found in God's Word.

Whether you're seeking spiritual growth, meaningful fellowship, or a place to serve, you'll find a warm welcome here. Our doors are open to all who wish to draw closer to God.
