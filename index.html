About Our Church

A family of believers rooted in faith

Nyaura SDA Church is a vibrant Seventh-day Adventist congregation committed to worshiping God in spirit and truth. For over 25 years, we've been a beacon of hope in our community.

We believe in the transformative power of faith, the importance of community, and the hope found in God's Word. Our doors are open to all who seek spiritual growth and meaningful fellowship.
